How BytesCrew Works

Direct student-to-student handover on your campus in four simple steps.

1

Verify Student ID

Confirm your college identity via college email OTP and student ID card for a safe campus environment.

2

Browse or List

Find course textbooks, calculators, drafters, or notes. Or list your own surplus gear in under 60 seconds.

3

Campus Chat

Message verified sellers directly in-app to ask questions, negotiate fair prices, and pick a handover spot.

4

Meet & Exchange

Meet at the library or campus canteen. Inspect the item in person before completing the UPI or cash payment.

Campus Trust & Safety

Every transaction is transparent, student-verified, and local to your university.

🪪

College ID Check

Every seller uploads their student ID card so you only interact with real enrolled classmates from your campus.

📧

Official Email Verification

A one-time OTP sent to the official .edu / .ac.in college email guarantees authentic student identity ownership.

🤝

Face-to-Face Handover

No postage scams, shipping delays, or parcel damage. Meet openly in public campus spots like the Library or Canteen.
